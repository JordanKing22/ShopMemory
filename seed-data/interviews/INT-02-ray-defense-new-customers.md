## T001 interviewer (scope) [SCOPE]
You're the expert here. I don't know your shop, so I'll ask a lot of how and why questions. Nothing becomes shop knowledge until you approve it. Should we focus on quoting new customers, or something else?

## T002 expert
New customers, and defense customers especially. The first lot is never priced like the second one.

## T003 interviewer (task_map) [TASK_MAP]
If you broke quoting a first lot for a new customer into three to six big steps, what would they be?

## T004 expert
Read their quality flow-downs. Figure out the first article and any source inspection. Price the parts like a normal job. Then add what the paperwork and the questions are going to cost you.

## T005 interviewer (task_map) [TASK_MAP]
Which step would you least want to hand to someone new, and why?

## T006 expert
The last one. The paperwork and the questions. You can't see it on the print.

## T007 interviewer (incident) [INCIDENT]
Think of a specific recent job where that step really mattered. Which job comes to mind?

## T008 expert
The Velmont housing. First lot for a brand-new customer, eight pieces, titanium, thin walls, full first article.

## T009 interviewer (timeline) [TIMELINE]
Walk me through that job from when it landed on your desk to when it shipped.

## T010 expert
I quoted it as a pilot. The parts, the first article, and a paperwork allowance on its own line. It still wasn't enough. They came back with two rounds of questions on the balloon drawing. Then their source inspector came out before we could ship. One housing got scrapped for a wall under minimum. It shipped late and way over the hours.

## T011 interviewer (timeline) [TIMELINE]
Here's what I have: you quoted it as a pilot with a paperwork allowance, then two rounds of questions, a source inspection visit, one housing scrapped for a wall under minimum, and it still shipped late and over the hours. What did I miss or get out of order?

## T012 expert
You got it. The scrap was the machining side. The rest was paperwork and waiting, a lot more of it than I'd allowed for.

## T013 interviewer (deepen) [ASK_WHY]
Why does the paperwork side cost so much more on a first lot?

## T014 expert
Nobody on either side knows the other one yet. Every question goes back and forth twice. The second lot, they know us and we know what they want.

## T015 interviewer (deepen) [ASK_CONDITION]
When does that apply? What has to be true about the customer?

## T016 expert
New customer, and a defense or space customer with a real quality department. A commercial customer with a simple PO, not so much.

## T017 interviewer (deepen) [KA_PROBE]
How do you handle that in the quote now?

## T018 expert
With a brand-new defense customer I usually quote the first lot as a pilot. I price the first article, the source inspection and a paperwork allowance as their own lines, and I tell them the second lot will come down. After Velmont I make the paperwork allowance bigger than feels right.

## T019 interviewer (deepen) [KA_PROBE]
Is there something about a particular customer that tripped someone up?

## T020 expert
Graymoor sends their source inspector for the first lot of any new part number. You have to schedule the job so the first articles are done a week before ship, or you're waiting on them.

## T021 interviewer (what_if) [WHAT_IF]
What if Velmont had been a repeat customer? Would your call have changed?

## T022 expert
Completely. Repeat customer, I price the parts and a normal first article. No pilot line, no big paperwork allowance.

## T023 interviewer (novice_gap) [NOVICE_GAP]
What would a newer quoter most likely get wrong here?

## T024 expert
They'd quote the first lot at the repeat price. The hours look the same on paper. And they'd forget to plan around the source inspection date.

## T025 interviewer (teach_back) [TEACH_BACK]
Let me say it back as a rule: with a brand-new defense customer, quote the first lot as a pilot, and price the first article, the source inspection and a paperwork allowance as their own lines. What did I get wrong or leave out?

## T026 expert
That's right. And tell them the second lot will come down, so they don't think the pilot price is the price.
