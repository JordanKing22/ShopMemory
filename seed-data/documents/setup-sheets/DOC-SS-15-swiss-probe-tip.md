## Workholding
- Ground 316 bar. Mike it in three places on a couple of bars from each bundle, then set the guide bushing to the bar you've got. [KC-047]

## Operations
- One op with pick-off (program O7560-10). Peck every drill and chip-break the roughing passes. [KC-048]
- Deburr and inspect, then out for passivation.

## Inspection
- Check surface finish on the working end of the first and last piece of every lot with the profilometer. [KC-043]
- Every working end goes under the scope, not a sample. [KC-043]

## Cautions
- No marker and no tape flags on the part. Sutrella rejects for residue. [KC-043]
- Don't let a tool make a continuous chip on 316. It drags across the finished tip. [KC-048]

## Checklist
- Bar miked and bushing set. [KC-047]
- First and last piece finish readings on the traveler. [KC-043]
- Parts bagged clean, no flags. [KC-043]
