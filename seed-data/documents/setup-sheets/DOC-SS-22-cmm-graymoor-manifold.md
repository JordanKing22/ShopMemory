- Manifold datum A down on the fixture plate rests; light clamps, never across a sealing face or a port. [KC-040]

## Operations
- Program CMM-4410-A is built so any feature runs by itself, named by its balloon number. [KC-044]
- Check sealing-face flatness after heat treat, not before. [KC-038]
- Scan the port bores, or take at least eight points at two depths. [KC-039]

## Inspection
- Before the source inspector arrives, lay out the first article parts, the ballooned drawing, the CMM reports and every cert. [KC-044]
- Expect the inspector to pick a few characteristics off the drawing and have us re-run them on the CMM while they watch. [KC-044]

## Cautions

- Drawings and reports stay in the inspection room while the inspector is here. [KC-044]
