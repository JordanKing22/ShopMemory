## Workholding
- Anything under 3/4 inch bar: put in the matching spindle liner before you load the feeder. [KC-059]
- Set the remnant length on the feeder for the bar you're running, and check it again before the last bar of the lot.

## Operations
- Spin the first bar up and watch. The lathe shaking when the bar spins up means the liner is wrong or missing. [KC-059]
- On 316, run a chip-breaking insert. [KC-060]

## Cautions
- Since the feeder went on in 2020, small bar whips in the spindle without the right liner. [KC-059]
- On 316, check the chip conveyor every hour. In 2022 it jammed overnight and burned out the conveyor motor. [KC-060]

## Checklist
- Liner matches the bar. [KC-059]
- Conveyor check on the hourly sheet for every 316 job. [KC-060]
