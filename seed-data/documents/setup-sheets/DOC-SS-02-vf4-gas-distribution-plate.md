## Workholding
- Op 10 in the VF-4 vise with clean soft jaws; op 20 flipped into soft jaws cut to the op 10 profile.
- Wipe the jaws and the vise down before every load. Chips pressed into a face end up in a port.

## Operations
- Op 10: face, profile and drill the gas hole pattern (program O1784-10).
- Op 20: flip, face to thickness and finish the two locating bores (program O1784-20).
- Deburr every port and bag the plate in the clean area before it leaves the machine. Any chip in a port is a reject. [KC-009]

## Inspection
- Blow out and look down every port under a light before the plate is bagged. [KC-009]
- The locating bores are tight. Let the plate soak on the granite to room temperature before they get measured. [KC-036]

## Cautions
- A plate that's off, even a missed chamfer or a floor a thou shallow: stop and tag it. No rework without Quantrel's written OK; Linda asks them first. [KC-029]
- A reworked plate they didn't approve is a reject even if it measures good. [KC-029]
- Never let finished plates sit in the open tote. [KC-009]

## Checklist
- Every port deburred and looked at. [KC-009]
- Plate bagged in the clean area at the machine. [KC-009]
- Any off plate tagged and handed to Linda, not re-cut. [KC-029]
