## Workholding
- From the bar feeder. Under 3/4 inch bar, the matching spindle liner goes in first. [KC-059]

## Operations
- Before you set up, check the rev: this pin is short for its diameter, so it runs here. If a new rev comes in longer than about three times its diameter in small bar, it goes on the Swiss. [KC-061]
- Turn and part off in one op (program O5296-10).
- Clean gloves from the part-off on; pins go straight into clean bags at the machine.

## Inspection
- Measure the pin diameters after the parts have cooled, not straight off the part-off.

## Cautions
- A pin that's off gets tagged and set aside. No rework on Quantrel parts without their written OK, not even a re-cut; Linda asks them. [KC-029]

- Off parts tagged, not re-cut. [KC-029]
- Rev checked against the Swiss-or-ST-20 rule. [KC-061]
