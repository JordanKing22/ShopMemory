## Workholding
- On 17-4 over two inches thick, set the flattest face down on the lower nozzle. [KC-027]
- Bring the upper nozzle right down to the part. [KC-027]
- Indicate the datum edge along the table bars before you pick up the start hole.

## Operations
- Run the first pass slower than the book on thick 17-4. [KC-027]
- Watch the first inch of the cut before you walk away.

## Cautions
- RP-M07's lower flushing has been weak since we bought it used in 2013. The cracked lower nozzle was replaced this January, and the flush on thick stock is still weak. [KC-027]
- When it breaks wire on thick 17-4, it's usually the flushing, not the wire. Don't change spools first. [KC-027]

## Checklist
- Flattest face down on the lower nozzle. [KC-027]
- Upper nozzle down to the part. [KC-027]
