## Workholding
- End cap datum A down on the fixture plate rests, light clamps well away from the bores. [KC-040]
- Photo of the setup is in the program folder; sit it the same way every lot. [KC-040]

## Operations
- Incoming: every part back from the heat treater gets its bores checked before it goes back on a machine (program CMM-2051-C, incoming routine). [KC-045]
- The bores moved out of round in heat treat on the first lot. That's what the incoming check is for. [KC-005]
- Final: full program CMM-2051-C after the finish op, aligned to the print datums, never to the faces the part was clamped on. [KC-037]

## Inspection
- Scan each bore, or take at least eight points at two depths. Never three. [KC-039]
- These bores are under a thou of total tolerance: soak the parts on the granite to room temperature before they're measured. [KC-036]

## Cautions
- Don't pass a bore on a three-point check. A bored hole can come out lobed, and three points can land right on the high spots. [KC-039]
- The incoming check goes on the schedule; don't squeeze it in. [KC-045]

## Checklist
- Incoming report in the job folder before op 30 starts. [KC-045]
- Bores scanned at incoming and at final. [KC-039]
- Parts soaked before either check. [KC-036]
