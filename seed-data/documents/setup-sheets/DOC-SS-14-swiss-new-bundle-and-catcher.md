## Workholding
- Mike the bar before you set the guide bushing: three places on a bar, a couple of bars from every new bundle. [KC-047]
- Set the bushing to the bar you've got, not to the size on the tag. [KC-047]
- If the bar varies more than about two tenths end to end it won't run right in the bushing. Use ground bar for anything tight. [KC-047]

## Operations
- Prove out the first part slow before you let it run.
- On 316, program a peck on every drill and a chip break on the roughing passes. [KC-048]
- Parts shorter than about a quarter inch overall: put the fine mesh liner in the chip pan under the catcher. [KC-050]

## Inspection
- Size wandering after a new bundle goes in: look at the bushing and the chips before you touch the offset. [KC-047, KC-048]

## Cautions
- RP-M06's catcher misses short parts. They drop into the chips and get dinged. [KC-050]
- Long, stringy 316 chips wrap the part and the tool and drag across the finished diameter. [KC-048]

## Checklist
- New bundle miked and the bushing reset to it. [KC-047]
- Catcher checked at every bar change. [KC-050]
- Liner in for short parts. [KC-050]
